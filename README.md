
Tabish — A Young Learner Building a Future

Tabish is a young student from India with a strong curiosity for technology, mathematics, science, business, and engineering. At an early age, he has developed an unusually broad set of interests and is actively working toward becoming highly skilled rather than depending only on formal education.

Currently studying in Class 6, Tabish is especially interested in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Mathematics, Computer Science, Physics, Engineering, and Chartered Accountancy (CA). His long-term goal is to build strong expertise across these areas and eventually use them together in meaningful projects and real-world work.

Education and Learning

Mathematics is one of Tabish’s strongest subjects. He has already studied a substantial amount of mathematical foundations, including:

- Basic arithmetic
- Fractions, ratios, and percentages
- Variables and equations
- Powers, exponents, and logarithms
- Functions
- Graphs and coordinates
- Slope and straight lines
- Domain and range
- Graph transformations
- Trigonometry
- Vectors and vector operations
- Vector magnitude/norm
- Dot products
- Matrices and matrix operations
- Matrix transpose
- Systems of linear equations
- Linear algebra concepts such as basis, span, and linear independence
- Introductory calculus and derivatives

He is continuing toward more advanced mathematics, including calculus, probability and statistics, optimization, differential equations, and additional linear algebra.

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

AI and ML are among Tabish’s major long-term interests. His planned learning path includes:

Python → Mathematics → Data → Machine Learning → Deep Learning → NLP → Computer Vision → Transformers → LLMs → Generative AI → AI Agents → Deployment → Projects

He already knows Python and is moving into scientific and AI programming with tools such as NumPy, pandas, and PyTorch.

He is particularly interested in understanding AI from the inside, rather than only using existing libraries. He wants to understand neural networks, weights, biases, activation functions, gradients, backpropagation, automatic differentiation, tokenization, transformer architecture, and language models.

One of his project ideas is to build a small GPT-style language model in Python/PyTorch, understand its architecture file-by-file and line-by-line, and eventually experiment with smaller language models, RAG systems, and AI applications.

Computer Science and Programming

Programming is another major part of Tabish’s skill development. He uses Python, VS Code, GitHub, and Google Colab, and is interested in understanding how software works rather than simply copying code.

He enjoys beginner-friendly but technically meaningful projects, including neural-network experiments, tokenization, data applications, graphics-related programming, and AI projects.

His preferred learning style is highly practical: understand the concept, see a simple example, write the code, and then gradually make the system more advanced.

Physics

Physics has recently become another major area of study.

Tabish wants to understand physics from the fundamentals instead of simply memorizing formulas. His planned core route is:

Basics → Vectors → Mechanics → Electricity & Magnetism → Waves → Optics → Thermodynamics → Modern Physics

He is especially interested in mechanics, including motion, vectors, forces, energy, momentum, gravitation, rotation, and fluid mechanics. Aerospace engineering has also caught his interest, making subjects such as mechanics, aerodynamics, thermodynamics, and orbital mechanics particularly relevant.

He sees physics as another way to understand how mathematics describes the real world.

Chartered Accountancy and Business

Alongside technology and science, Tabish is also interested in Chartered Accountancy, accounting, finance, and business.

He has already explored a significant portion of accounting fundamentals, including topics such as debit and credit rules, journal entries, ledgers, trial balance, trading accounts, profit and loss accounts, balance sheets, bank reconciliation, rectification of errors, depreciation, interest, and partnership-related concepts.

He is interested in learning commerce, economics, and accounting well before reaching the formal stages of CA education.

Engineering

Engineering connects many of Tabish’s interests.

His interests in mathematics, physics, programming, AI/ML, and computer science naturally lead toward engineering disciplines. Aerospace engineering is one area that interests him, while AI engineering and computer-related engineering are also closely connected to his goals.

He is interested not only in learning theories but in eventually building things.

Other Skills

Tabish has also developed skills outside his main academic interests.

He has experience with:

- Graphic design
- Coding
- Excel
- Typing, including a measured speed of around 64 WPM
- GitHub and development tools
- Digital design and visual work
- Practical exposure to accounting/business software

He has also experimented with freelance graphic-design work and has already earned money from design projects.

Learning Philosophy

Tabish tends to think in terms of long-term mastery rather than short-term memorization.

He prefers:

Deep understanding → practice → projects → revision → real-world application

He also likes learning roadmaps that clearly show what is completed, what is currently being studied, and what comes next.

He frequently asks not only “What is this?” but also “Why is this needed?”, “How does it work internally?”, and “How does it connect to something else?”

Personal Development

Discipline and consistency are major personal goals for Tabish. He wants to become more consistent with studying, learning, habits, and long-term projects.

He is interested in systems such as structured schedules, habit-building, revision, and focused study periods. He wants to balance school with his independent learning rather than replacing school with it.

The Bigger Picture

Tabish’s interests can be summarized as a connected learning ecosystem:

Mathematics gives him the language of quantitative reasoning.

Physics helps him understand the physical world.

Computer Science teaches him how computation and software work.

AI/ML allows him to build intelligent systems.

Engineering connects theory with real-world systems and machines.

CA and business develop his understanding of accounting, finance, and how organizations operate.

His long-term ambition is therefore not limited to being good at one school subject. He wants to build a broad technical and professional foundation, combining scientific thinking, mathematics, programming, artificial intelligence, engineering, and business knowledge.

At his current stage, he is still learning the foundations—but he is already deliberately building toward much more advanced work.

Core Focus Areas

Physics • CA • AI/ML • Mathematics • Computer Science • Engineering

These are the six areas he currently considers the central pillars of his long-term learning journey.
